## 📚 Online Book store Management & Data Analysis (SQL Project)
Yeh project PostgreSQL ka use karke ek Online Bookstore Database ko design karne aur uske upar various basic se lekar advanced SQL queries execute karke meaningful insights nikalne ke liye banaya gaya hai.

## 🛠️ Database Schema & Structure
  Is project mein teen main tables banayi gayi hain:
 * Books: Books ki details jaise Book_ID, Title, Author, Genre, Published_Year, Price, aur Stock store karta hai.
 * Customers: Customers ki personal details jaise Customer_ID, Name, Email, Phone, City, aur Country store karta hai.
 * Orders: Har ek order ka transaction record rakhta hai jisme Order_ID, Customer_ID, Book_ID, Order_Date, Quantity, aur Total_Amount shamil hain.

## 📊 Analysis & Queries Performed
## 🌱 Basic Questions:

 * Retrieve all books in the "Fiction" genre: Sabhi fiction books ki details nikalna.
 * Find books published after the year 1950: 1950 ke baad publish hone wali books find karna.
 * List all customers from the China: China ke sabhi customers ki list.
 * Show orders placed in November 2023: November 2023 ke orders dekhna.
 * Retrieve the total stock of books available: Books ka total available stock calculate karna.
 * Find the details of the most expensive book: Sabse mehngi book ki details nikalna.
 * Show all customers who ordered more than 1 quantity of a book: Aise customers ko dekhna jinhone 1 se zyada quantity order ki hai.
 * Retrieve all orders where the total amount exceeds $20: Jinka total amount $20 se zyada ho.
 * List all genres available in the Book table: Sabhi unique genres ki list.
 * Find the book with the lowest stock: Sabse kam stock wali book find karna.
 * Calculate the total revenue generated for all orders: Sabhi orders se total revenue calculate karna.
## 🚀 Advance Questions:
 * Retrieve the total number of books sold for each genre: Har genre mein kitni books sold hui hain.
 * Find the average price of books in the "Fantasy" genre: Fantasy genre ki books ka average price find karna.
 * List customers who have placed at least 2 orders: Jinhone kam se kam 2 orders place kiye hain.
 * Find the most frequently ordered book: Sabse zyada baar order hone wali book.
 * Show the top 3 most expensive books of 'Fantasy' genre: Fantasy genre ki top 3 expensive books.
 * Retrieve the total quantity of books sold by each author: Har author dwara sold total quantity.
 * List the cities where customers who spent over $30 are located: Aise cities ki list jahan customers ne $30 ya zyada spend kiya hai.
 * Find the customer who spent the most on orders: Sabse zyada paisa spend karne wala customer.
 * Calculate the stock remaining after fulfilling all orders: Orders fulfill hone ke baad remaining stock calculate karna.
## 💻 Tools & language
